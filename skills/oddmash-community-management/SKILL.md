# Oddmash community management

Read the organization's community standards, repository contribution guide, templates, and relevant conversation. External contributions are voluntary; do not imply recruitment, employment, payment, or roadmap ownership.

- Triage issues by reproducibility, user impact, scope, and available evidence. Distinguish bug, feature request, documentation, and support; use existing templates and labels. Ask for only the missing information needed to advance.
- For new contributors, propose a bounded genuinely useful task with expected behavior, relevant files or docs, and verification criteria. Avoid busywork and preserve the contributor's authorship and existing branch.
- Communicate in concise, respectful English for public issues, PRs, and reviews. Acknowledge contributions, explain concrete requested changes and their reasons, and distinguish blockers from optional improvements.
- Keep private conversations and personal details out of public replies. Never promise timelines, access, roles, compensation, or merge outcomes without maintainer authorization. Draft outbound messages unless sending or posting is explicitly requested.
- Record unresolved decisions and next actions without creating duplicate issues or excessive notifications. Escalate sensitive reports to the maintainers through the repository's documented process.

Use [quality assurance](../oddmash-quality-assurance/SKILL.md) for verification criteria and [product growth](../oddmash-product-growth/SKILL.md) for aggregated feedback.
